**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including source code, documentation,
translations, test data, configuration, designs and any modification or addition to an
existing work, that is intentionally Submitted by You to the Licensor for inclusion in,
or documentation of, the Project.

**"Submit"** and **"Submitted"** mean any form of electronic, verbal or written
communication sent to the Licensor or its representatives for the purpose of discussing
or improving the Project, including pull requests, patches, issues and messages on the
Project's issue tracker, mailing lists and chat channels, but excluding communication
that is conspicuously marked or otherwise designated in writing by You as "Not a
Contribution".

**"Project"** means Tenko DDI: the software, documentation and related material in the
repository published by the Licensor, in any version, and derivative works of it made
by or for the Licensor.

**"Designated Contributor"** means an employee, officer or contractor of the Entity whom
the Entity has named in Schedule 1 or by a later notice under B.2.

**"Licensor"** means the party identified in Part A or Part B, its successors and
permitted assigns.

## Part D: How to accept the Agreement, and how acceptance is recorded

Acceptance is required once per person (Part A) or once per entity (Part B) before the
first pull request is merged. It is recorded in the pull request and in the git history,
so the record is public and travels with the code.

### D.1 Individuals

1. Read this Agreement in full.
2. In the description of your first pull request, include this sentence, with your real
   name (no pseudonyms, no anonymous contributions):

   > I have read the Tenko DDI Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0, and I accept
   > it as an individual contributor. Name: [Full name].

3. Add a `Signed-off-by: Full Name <email@example.com>` line to every commit you submit,
   now and in the future (`git commit -s` adds it from your git configuration). The name
   and email must be the ones in your acceptance statement and in the commit's author
   field. Each `Signed-off-by` line is your certification that the commit is a
   Contribution covered by this Agreement and that the representations in A.4 and A.5
   hold for it.

### D.2 Entities

1. An authorised representative completes Schedule 1 and sends it by email to the
   contact below, from the representative's own address at the Entity.
2. The Licensor acknowledges the acceptance by email. The Agreement is effective on the
   date of that acknowledgement, and the Licensor keeps the Schedule and the exchange of
   emails as the record.
3. Each Designated Contributor includes this sentence in the description of their first
   pull request:

   > My contributions are made on behalf of [Entity name], which has accepted the Tenko
   > DDI Entity Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0. Name: [Full name].

4. Each Designated Contributor adds the `Signed-off-by` line described in D.1 step 3 to
   every commit.

### D.3 The record

- The acceptance statement in the pull request and the `Signed-off-by` lines in the git
  history are the primary record. Maintainers preserve `Signed-off-by` lines when
  merging and do not merge commits without them.
- The Licensor keeps a register of accepted agreements (name or entity, version, date
  and the pull request or email reference) and the completed Schedules of entities.
- A pull request from someone without an accepted Agreement, or without `Signed-off-by`
  lines, is not merged. Maintainers may ask for the statement again if the record is
  unclear.
