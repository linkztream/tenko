- The coalescer does what ADR 0002 decision 4 needs: on the leader every Raft entry costs one fsynced log commit (log commits equal the Raft entries, within 2, in every window, because openraft 0.9 waits for each flush), and the store answers by putting more batches into each entry as the load rises, from 5.1 batches per entry at 1,000/s to 32.2 at 5,000/s and 45.3 in the closed loop (deferred durability).
- Immediate durability fsyncs the state machine as well, so fewer entries complete per second; it compensates with larger entries (12.2 to 155.0 batches per entry) and still sustains 1,000 and 2,000 updates/s, but at 5,000/s it falls behind (4,708 proposals outstanding at the end of the window). Deferred durability, the default, came within 1 percent at 5,000/s (4,964.6/s achieved, 1,058 outstanding at the end of the window, drained in 0.1 s).
- The median latency at 1,000/s is 20.3 ms with deferred durability and 51.9 ms with immediate durability. The p99 is 289.9 ms and 167.4 ms. The checkpoint-interval runs (p99 151.0 ms at 250 ms, 289.9 ms at 1 s, 190.3 ms at 10 s) do not show a monotonic effect, and the machine was busy with other builds during these windows (CPU peaks at 100 percent), so the tail is not attributed to the checkpoint here. ADR 0002 expects dynamic-update latency of 50 to 200 ms under heavy load.
