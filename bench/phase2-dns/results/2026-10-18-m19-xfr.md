# M19: IXFR delta and NOTIFY latency, a 100-record change on a 100,000-record zone (Linux musl, one Docker host)

Verdict: PASS (in five rounds a batch of 100 changed records on a zone of 100,000 records reached BIND 9 in 649 to 721 ms from the commit, median 689 ms, against the 5 s threshold; BIND had the NOTIFY 233 to 237 ms after the commit, and every transfer was an IXFR of 204 records and 4,399 octets in one message, the change only, where the full zone took 144 messages, 100,003 records and 2,306,779 octets)

M19 of the Phase 2 plan (section 6) asks that a 100-record change reaches BIND within 5 s and that the delta carries only the change. This run measures WP6.1 (commit `859f6dd`): NOTIFY out from the leader service `dns.notify`, IXFR out from the journal on the TCP listener of the `dns` role, TSIG on every message, BIND 9 as the secondary. Script: `scripts/run-m19-xfr.sh` (raw log `results/raw/m19-20261018-173938.log`, not committed). Every figure is a single-node figure (plan section 2.10).

Machine: Intel Core i9-13900K (24 cores, 32 threads), 63.7 GiB RAM, Windows 11 Enterprise 10.0.26300; the lab in WSL2 on the same host (Ubuntu 24.04.3, kernel 6.18.33.2-microsoft-standard-WSL2, 51 GiB given to the VM), Docker 29.1 as root. Tenko: the image of `deploy/Dockerfile` built from `859f6dd`, which holds the static musl binary that ships (`x86_64-unknown-linux-musl`, release). BIND: `internetsystemsconsortium/bind9:9.20` (9.20.29). Tenko, BIND and the client are containers on one bridge network on one host, so these are loopback figures: an upper bound of what the software does, without the delay of a real link.

## Procedure

1. The compose DNS lab (`deploy/compose/lab-dns`) runs its transfer round with `KEEP=1`: Tenko set up through its API under the Lab profile, the TSIG key of the run (HMAC-SHA256) in Tenko and in BIND, an access list naming the key. All eleven rows of the round passed.
2. `big.example.test` is created on Tenko as a primary zone with `require_tsig`, the access list and BIND as its NOTIFY target, and 100,000 A records are imported from a zone file through the API (the import job: 12.0 s). BIND's secondary zone of the same name (`transfer.conf`) takes it by AXFR: 144 messages, 100,003 records, 2,306,779 octets in 0.28 s.
3. Five rounds: one batch of 100 records (a new address for each of `h0` to `h99`) through `POST /dns/zones/{id}/records/batch`. From the moment the API answered (the change set committed, with its serial step and journal entry) the script asks BIND every 20 ms for `h99.big.example.test` until it has the new address. BIND's log, read with Docker's timestamps, gives the moment the NOTIFY arrived and the size of the transfer.

## Results

| Round | Commit to BIND answer (ms) | NOTIFY at BIND (ms after the commit) | Transfer to BIND | Delta only |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 689 | 237 | IXFR, 1 message, 204 records, 4,399 octets, 0.019 s | yes |
| 2 | 686 | 234 | IXFR, 1 message, 204 records, 4,399 octets, 0.019 s | yes |
| 3 | 690 | 233 | IXFR, 1 message, 204 records, 4,399 octets, 0.031 s | yes |
| 4 | 721 | 235 | IXFR, 1 message, 204 records, 4,399 octets, 0.035 s | yes |
| 5 | 649 | 237 | IXFR, 1 message, 204 records, 4,399 octets, 0.019 s | yes |

- **The delta.** 204 records are the change only: the new SOA, the old SOA, the 100 records removed, the new SOA, the 100 records added, and the new SOA that closes the answer (RFC 1995 section 4). The full zone is 2.3 MB; the delta is 0.2 percent of it.
- **Where the time goes.** About 235 ms pass before BIND has the NOTIFY: the 250 ms the service gathers a burst of changes before it notifies (`NotifySettings::coalesce`), minus the moment between the commit and the API's answer, plus the wait until the role's index serves the commit. The rest, about 450 ms, is BIND's: its SOA query, the IXFR and the update of its copy, and the 20 ms polling of the script. Shortening the coalescing window would cut the first part; it exists so that a burst of changes sends one NOTIFY.
- **An earlier run of this procedure** before `859f6dd` stalled at the initial transfer (BIND did not take the imported serial within 300 s; the next run passed). The likely cause, found in the code: NOTIFY went out when the store committed, so BIND's SOA query could reach the role before its index had applied the 100,000-record import, see the old serial and stop until its next refresh. NOTIFY out now waits until the role of the process serves the commit (`ServedRevision`).

## Reproduce

```sh
IMAGE=tenko:lab bash bench/phase2-dns/scripts/run-m19-xfr.sh            # 100,000 records, 100 changes, 5 rounds
IMAGE=tenko:lab bash bench/phase2-dns/scripts/run-m19-xfr.sh --rounds 1
```

As root on a Linux Docker host, from the repository root; without `IMAGE` the lab smoke builds `tenko:lab` first.
