# M37: the cache with the new entry layout, M24 again (Windows)

Verdict: PASS (the hit ratio is the Phase 2 figure to the fourth digit: 0.8371 at the 100,000-entry bound in all six runs against 0.837, and 1.0000 once every question fits; the paced p99 at the bound is 1,147 to 1,371 us in four runs of the new layout against 1,244 and 1,448 us of `main` in the same hour, so the change costs nothing measurable; against the Phase 2 figure of 1.2 ms it is between 8 percent lower and 10 percent higher, the spread this host showed between runs today for both binaries; no timeout and no error in any pass)

M37 of the recursion plan (section 6) runs M24 of Phase 2 again after WP10.0 changed the answer cache: the key lost the client's DO and CD bits, the entry gained its validation state, the positions of its DNSSEC records, the extended error and the signature expiry, a hit renders the entry for the client's flags ([dns-contracts.md](../../../docs/dns-contracts.md) section 11), and the forwarder asks its upstreams with DO. The threshold is the hit ratio and the p99 within 5 percent of the Phase 2 figures (0.837 at the 100,000-entry bound; p99 1.2 ms paced at 20,000 queries per second). The procedure is the Phase 2 one, unchanged: [2026-10-18-m24-cache.md](2026-10-18-m24-cache.md) has the query mix, the passes and how to read them. Every figure is a single-node, loopback figure.

## The same hour, `main` against the new layout

Because the host's figures moved between runs by more than the threshold, the cache bound of 100,000 entries was measured four times in a row, alternating the release build of `main` (commit `8fab1f8`, built from a clean export with the same web UI) and that of the WP10.0 tree, with no compiler running.

| Binary | Closed loop QPS | Closed loop p99 us | Paced p50 us | Paced p99 us | Paced p99.9 us | Hit ratio (paced) | Stale answers in the cut |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `main` | 156,133 | 2,396.2 | 571.4 | 1,447.9 | 2,077.7 | 0.8371 | 598,135 |
| WP10.0 | 223,888 | 1,790.0 | 511.2 | 1,355.8 | 2,015.2 | 0.8371 | 596,436 |
| `main` | 188,863 | 2,672.6 | 525.3 | 1,244.2 | 1,758.2 | 0.8371 | 600,786 |
| WP10.0 | 233,333 | 968.7 | 485.4 | 1,146.9 | 1,646.6 | 0.8371 | 596,771 |

The new layout is not slower: its paced p99 averages 1,251 us against 1,346 us for `main`, and its closed loop answered more queries per second in both pairs. The hit path does more than before (the client's flags, the state's rendering rule, the check for DNSSEC records to leave out), but for the unsigned answers of this zone none of it touches the octets copied, and the counting-allocator test proves it allocates nothing. The entries asked with DO by the upstream query cost no space here: the example server's zone is unsigned, so the answers carry no signatures. A signed zone makes its entries larger by its RRSIGs; that is M39's to measure with the lab root.

## Not measured here

- **Linux and the musl binary**, for the reason of the Phase 2 run (the WSL2 loopback relay of this host); the Linux figure is WP10.5's on the lab VMs (plan section 6, M37 final).
- **Signed answers, the hit for a client without DO and the bogus hit** under load: the rendering is covered for function and allocation by `crates/tenko-dns/src/cache/render_tests.rs` and `tests/alloc.rs`; M38 measures validation.
