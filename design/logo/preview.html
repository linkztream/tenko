Tenko mark

A nine-tailed fox, the form a tenko takes. Every variant on fixed light and dark surfaces.

One color (currentColor)

The default. It inherits the text color, so it follows the theme without a second file. Below 24 px the small cut is used (no eyes, wider gap). Files: tenko-mark.svg, tenko-favicon.svg.

Two-tone

Tails in the accent color, head in currentColor. For the sign-in screen, the wizard and the README; not below 32 px. File: tenko-mark-duo.svg.

Favicon test at 16 and 32 px

The small cut rendered into a real 16 and 32 pixel bitmap, then enlarged without smoothing, so you see exactly the pixels a browser tab shows. The favicon file switches between ink and light ink with the system theme.

Tenko DDI
Tenko DDI

Lockup

Mark plus the wordmark "Tenko" and a smaller "DDI". The wordmark is drawn, not typeset: monoline strokes on the mark's grid, so it needs no font file. "DDI" sits exactly in the x-height of "Tenko", in a lighter stroke at 62 % opacity. File: tenko-lockup.svg.

Construction

Grid
48 by 48 units. At 16 px one unit is a third of a pixel; at 48 px it is one pixel.
Head
A seven-point polygon: two ears, wide cheeks, a pointed chin. 18 units wide and 20.7 units tall, symmetric about x = 24. The eyes are two slanted cut-outs, dropped below 24 px.
Tails
Nine tails fan out from F (24, 33), just behind the chin, at 25-degree steps from -100 to +100 degrees. Each follows a curved center line, is widest just outside the head and flicks its tip outward by 18 degrees. Lengths run from 22 units at the sides to 24 at the center (the two circles).
Gap
A 1.5-unit knockout around the head (dashed amber) separates head and tails in one color. The favicon cut widens it to 1.9 units.
Color
One color, always solid. The two-tone variant uses the accent for the tails only. No gradients, outlines or shadows.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a clear space of x on every side, where x is half the width of the head (9 units, about a fifth of the mark's height). Nothing else (text, edges, other marks) enters that zone. Minimum sizes: mark 16 px (small cut below 24 px), lockup 88 px wide.
