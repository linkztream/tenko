Mockup notes: dashboard

Try it: switch Mockup scene in the status bar (Needs attention, All clear, Standard with three nodes), switch System activity off in Recent changes, press N, pick a tenant and VRF in Fullest prefixes, and press Show all metrics in the Hidden line. Every address, MAC and name is a documentation or placeholder value.

  1. Decided: five blocks in reading order instead of 24 equal cards: needs attention, the health row, the DNS and DHCP panels, the fullest prefixes beside the recent changes, and one Hidden line. A conflict is the first thing on the page.
  2. Decided: the strip has one row per finding to act on (glyph, kind, subject, one line of evidence, since when, the default action) and one line for the findings to review, counted per area. With nothing to act on it is one All clear line, never two empty cards. Only Act now colors a surface; Review colors the glyph and the word; Info is never colored.
  3. Decided: the health row carries counts and one line of context each, every block a link. Jobs and Connectors belong to the Standard and Hardened sets; the Home set names them in the Hidden line instead.
  4. Decided: a panel is healthy as a glyph and a word, never a green fill. DHCP shows one empty state with one action while no scope has a pool, and its figures take over once a pool exists.
  5. Decided: free disk and store size sit in the page subtitle; low disk becomes an Act now finding. The controller's metrics (commit p99, replication lag, node buffers, live clients) live on Cluster and come back here under Lab or with more than one node.
  6. Decided: Recent changes reads through the label catalog, with the code one line below in the data font. Repeats of the same action by the same principal are one row with a count (x2). System activity (what Tenko did itself) is on under Home and Lab and off under Standard and Hardened.
  7. Decided: the alarm threshold is said once in the panel header when every row shares it; the meters keep a hairline at the threshold. Utilization has one decimal only under 10 %.
  8. Not shown: the Hardened security panel (security events and an audit summary of the last 24 hours) and the live tiles of every metric behind Show all metrics.
