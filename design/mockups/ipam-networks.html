    Mockup notes: IPAM networks

    Try it: switch Mockup scene in the status bar or click a network in the tree, hover or arrow through the address map, press / or use the chips above the table, open or close the supernet with its chevron. Every address here comes from the documentation ranges.

    1. Decided: the tree shows supernets and top-level networks with a child count and never unfolds by itself. Only the person opens a node; here 203.0.113.0/24 is open and the others are closed.
    2. Decided: the header carries one utilization meter, in use of the usable addresses, neutral until the alarm level is reached. The alarm line sits at the warning threshold and the scale line names it.
    3. Decided: the address map colors only findings. Act now and Review fill the cell and carry the last octet in the paired -on token; Info is a ring. In use, held by a range, free and not usable are quiet fills, the last two hatched.
    4. Decided: a supernet lists its networks in one table, fullest first. "Active" is said once in the Status header and the cells stay empty. No VLAN and No description are chips with counts.
    5. Decided: above 1,024 addresses the map draws one cell per /24 with its in-use count; a /16 is 256 cells. A prefix of more than 1,024 blocks has no map.
