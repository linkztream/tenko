Mockup notes: jobs

Try it: switch Mockup scene in the status bar between Calm and Running, click a group row to open or close it, and use "Show the rest of yesterday". The chips and the filter field work on the rows that are loaded.

  1. Decided: the default view leads with jobs that run or wait, then lists finished jobs by day. Nothing is split into tabs.
  2. Decided: when nothing is active there is one calm line above the table, not a card. The finished jobs stay below it.
  3. Decided: a done job shows its green only in the small glyph; the word stays in ink and no row or column is filled. Failed and waiting are the exceptions that carry color.
  4. Decided: a job that waits for a person gets a 3 px edge in the act color and its own group row, so it cannot be missed.
  5. Decided: the Steps column says "3 of 4" while a job is not over and the duration as people say it ("2 min 10 s") once it is done.
  6. Decided: the Started by column appears only when more than one person has started jobs; until then the filter bar says "Started by · all you".
  7. Decided: every row, every group row and the "Show the rest" row is 28 px.
