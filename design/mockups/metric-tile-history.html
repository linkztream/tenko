Today and proposed, at the real tile size

Both tiles are 283 px wide, a controller tile in a 1280 px window. Today's sparkline forgets after 60 s. The proposed tile draws the last two days on one continuous axis: the newest seconds at an even pace on the right, the past compressed more and more toward the left, with no border anywhere. The grid lines are moments in time; they glide left as time passes and close up as they go, and they fade in and out as their spacing changes. Each bucket keeps its lowest and highest value, so a burst of five seconds hours ago is still a needle. Point at the chart, or tab to it and use the arrow keys.

Mockup notes: metric history

Try it: pick "5 s burst", set Reach to 7 d and see the needle stay; compare the chosen axis with the earlier designs below; pick Restart and switch between Memory only and Small file; set Scope to Cluster of 3; switch Motion and watch the meter; press "Mark the last minute"; tab to a chart and press the arrow keys, Home and End; switch to Dashboard in the header, where "Pause motion" stops every chart; switch the theme in the top bar. Every name is a placeholder; the data is generated in the page and is not a measurement.

  1. Decided (DH-Q1), the owner on 2026-11-11: one continuous axis, no zones; the grid scrolls left and compresses as it goes. The earlier designs A and B stay in the comparison for reference.
  2. Motion: the bar at the top measures what the drawing costs in this browser ("Drawing: ..."); switch between 10 and 30 frames a second and stepping once a second, and hide the tab to see the drawing stop. Add ?bench=1 to the address to measure 300 frames in a row, and use window.tenkoHistory.pause(true) and frameAt(t) in the browser console to draw any moment and read what was drawn.
  3. Open (DH-Q2): how far back. 1 h, 2 d or 7 d. Recommended: 2 d (70 × 1 s, 60 × 10 s, 60 × 1 min, 36 × 10 min, 43 × 1 h), so today's rhythm can be held against yesterday's.
  4. Open (DH-Q3): across a restart. Memory only (history starts again, drawn hatched with the restart marked; in a cluster that controller is missing from the past, a burst included) or a small file per controller (after a clean stop only the downtime is missing). Recommended: memory only for the first release.
  5. Open (DH-Q4): which tiles first. All 25 at once, or DNS and host first. Recommended: all at once (the Dashboard view).
  6. Open (DH-Q5): the cluster's band. An envelope from each controller's own history (sums of the lowests and of the highests, which contain the true range) or exact cluster history kept every second. Recommended: the envelope; the readout says what each number is.
  7. Open (DH-Q6): the scale. The highest in view, or with lone needles cut and marked with a caret. Recommended: cut lone needles (switch Scale to compare).
  8. Open (DH-Q7): motion on the dashboard. Every tile on screen glides at 10 frames a second, only the tile under the pointer, or every tile steps once a second. Recommended: every tile glides, with the fallback to a step a second on a slow device and "Pause motion" on the header.
  9. Built in the mockup only: the chart holds still while the pointer or the keyboard is on it ("Held"), the number keeps updating; the readout is the same for the pointer and the keyboard and is announced to screen readers.
  10. Colors: the data is --series-1 (as the DNS panel's chart), the band the same hue as a wash with its upper edge drawn, so a needle one pixel wide still shows; the accent marks only the bucket under the pointer and the focus. Today's sparkline is drawn in the accent; the plan proposes moving it to --series-1.
