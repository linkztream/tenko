Phase 1: Tenko changes IPAM intent and UniFi, then waits until each device shows up in the new network. Reservation and DNS changes, and joining a Wi-Fi on a device, are manual tasks you tick off.

Wireless networks: SSIDs are IPAM objects, read from UniFi with their VLAN. Home carries VLAN 10 (LAN), Home-IoT carries VLAN 30 (IoT), Lab-Test carries VLAN 20 (Lab). A wireless device moves to the SSID that carries the target VLAN, so it has to join that SSID.

The job creates these tasks when it starts, in one checklist for the whole job. A move starts when its DHCP and DNS tasks are ticked and a slot is free. Device tasks (reconfigure a printer, join a Wi-Fi) are not part of that gate: Tenko waits for the device afterwards. Clean-up tasks (remove the old reservation and old DNS records) are added after each device is seen in the new network.

    Mockup notes: move job

    Switch scenes in the status bar. Preview: untick a device (or press Space on a focused row) and watch the counts and the button change; switch a wireless row between the SSID move and a per-client VLAN override; Ctrl+Enter starts. Running (always the same sample job, 2 in parallel, 12 h timeout): wait about 8 seconds, tick printer-1's tasks, try Retry or Roll back on speaker-den (its Wi-Fi join was refused), open a device's steps with its chevron. Jobs list: filter, search, start a scan, open #221.

    1. Owner's request: wireless networks (SSIDs) are IPAM objects; a wireless move goes to the SSID that carries the target VLAN instead of a per-client VLAN override.
    2. Open question on SSIDs: a controller cannot switch a client to another SSID, so someone has to join Home-IoT on the device. How should Tenko handle devices that cannot be reconfigured remotely (no screen, no app, Wi-Fi set once at install)? Skip them, hold the move until you confirm, or fall back to the per-client VLAN override?
    3. printer-1 is static, so it needs no reservation. To reach the 10 manual DHCP and DNS tasks (5 DHCP, 5 DNS) I gave it a task to keep 198.51.100.30 out of the DHCP pool. Is that a real task, or should the count read 9?
    4. A move is done when the device is seen. Clean-up (old reservation, old DNS records) is a separate task that stays open in the checklist and does not hold the move. Should the move stay open until clean-up is ticked?
    5. A waiting move holds a parallelism slot until it is seen, fails or times out. With parallelism 2 and a 12 h timeout, one silent device can block half the capacity for half a day. Should waiting moves release their slot?
    6. Pause stops new moves from starting; moves in progress keep their observation clock running, so the 12 h timeouts keep counting. Is that what you expect, or should Pause stop the clock too?
    7. Per row you can change the address (Choose another address on speaker-den) and, on wireless rows, the method (SSID move or per-client VLAN override). Do you also want to edit the new hostname per device here?
    8. With 60 devices the device list gets long. Group by state with counts and fold the done ones, or keep one flat list with a state filter?
