Mockup notes: shell and IPAM

Try it: tick rows (Shift-click for a range), use the arrow keys, press Enter on a hostname or mode, drag selected rows onto a network or an SSID in the tree, press Ctrl+K or ?. The account menu (AK, top right) has the "Emphasize host part" preference.

  • Decided: dragging onto the prefix tree is the primary move gesture; M and the action bar stay as equals. The move preview is its own page.
  • Decided: addresses and MACs render plain in the data font. Graying the network part and bolding the host part is a per-user preference, off by default.
  • Decided: compact rows (28 px) by default; the rail keeps icons with labels (72 px); the status bar stays; the start page is the dashboard.
  • Owner’s request: wireless networks (SSIDs) are IPAM objects, read from UniFi with their VLAN mapping and shown under the network that carries their VLAN (Home under LAN, Home-IoT under IoT, Lab-Test under Lab). Dropping wireless clients on an SSID moves them to that SSID’s network.
  1. Free runs are collapsed into one striped row. Visible by default (Show free), or hidden with a counter?
  2. Source badges: seven possible. A text badge per source, or a compact "4 of 5 agree" with details on hover?
  3. VLAN is a column although the prefix implies it; it earns its place only when observation differs (speaker-den shows "10 → 30"). Hide it by default?
  4. The DHCP pool is one collapsed range row. List its leases inline when expanded, or only in the DHCP view?
  5. Should an SSID row expand to list its clients (from UniFi), or only link to a filtered grid?
