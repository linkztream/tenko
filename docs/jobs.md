# Jobs

Every change that touches more than one row runs as a job (IPAM-17): a move, an import, an edit of IPAM, DNS or DHCP, a scan, an update. The API is `GET /api/v1/jobs` and `GET /api/v1/jobs/{id}`, with `POST /api/v1/jobs/{id}/cancel` and `POST /api/v1/jobs/{id}/steps/{index}/complete` for the two things a person can do to a job; the `jobs` event topic announces each change (API-09).

## The jobs list

The list at `/jobs` answers "is anything running or waiting for me?" first. Its default chip, **Active and needs you**, leads with the jobs that are not over: the ones with a task waiting for a person ("Waiting for you", in the colour of a finding to act on, with an edge on the row) above the ones that run, are queued or roll back ("In progress"). Below them the finished jobs are grouped by the local day they finished on, "Today · 12 done", "Yesterday · 25 done" and "Earlier", Today and Yesterday open and Earlier closed; a day shows its first ten jobs and a "Show the rest of yesterday" row, and the oldest day offers "Show older jobs" when the server has more than the 200 read, so a closed group never pulls the whole history in. With nothing running or waiting, one line says so above the days ("Nothing is running and no job waits for you. Finished jobs are below."). The other chips are **Failed or rolled back** and **Finished** (finished jobs only, by day) and **All**; the query field filters by title, kind or id, and `?kind=`, `?target=` and `?group=` narrow the list as before (`?view=needs` opens the default). Each row shows the State as a glyph and a word (colour only for Failed and Waiting for you; a Done row has a green glyph and no green word or fill), the Job by its title from the label catalog (`jobs.title.<kind>` when the server's title key has no message of its own) with its host and, in muted ink, how many objects it changes unless the title already says it, the Kind as the code, the Steps ("3 of 4", or for a job that is done how long it took from submission to end, "2 min 10 s"), the Current step only for jobs that run or wait, and when it finished, relative with the exact time on hover. Started by is a column only while two people started the listed jobs; when one did, the filter bar says it once ("Started by · all you"). The header's facts ("0 running · 0 waiting for you · 200+ finished") take the waiting count from the findings store, the number of the rail's Jobs badge. A job's own page (`/jobs/<id>`) keeps its steps, tasks and log under the same page header as the list.
